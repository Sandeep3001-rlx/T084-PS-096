# T084-PS-096 Business Rule Register

## Project
Agri Mandi Electronic Auction and Commission Settlement Engine

Team ID: T084  
Language: Python 3.12.7

## A. Registration and Quality Grading

### BR-01: Unique Identifiers
Every farmer, commission agent, trader, commodity, lot, auction, and settlement must have a unique identifier within its entity type.

### BR-02: Trader Credit Limit
A trader's credit limit is equal to ten times the trader's security deposit.

Formula: Credit Limit = 10 × Security Deposit.

### BR-03: Grade A
A lot receives Grade A if all three conditions are satisfied:
- Moisture ≤ 12%.
- Foreign matter ≤ 1%.
- Damage ≤ 2%.

### BR-04: Grade B
If the lot does not qualify for Grade A, it receives Grade B when all three conditions are satisfied:
- Moisture ≤ 14%.
- Foreign matter ≤ 2%.
- Damage ≤ 4%.

Otherwise, the lot receives Grade C.

### BR-05: Reference Price
The reference price depends on the grade:
- Grade A: 100% of MSP.
- Grade B: 97% of MSP.
- Grade C: 92% of MSP.

### BR-06: Lot Registration
Each lot must retain its farmer, commodity, declared weight, assay results, grade, reference price, applicable agent, and current status.

## B. Auction and Bidding

### BR-07: Initial Price Floor
The initial auction floor is 90% of the lot's reference price.

### BR-08: Bid Floor Validation
A bid below the applicable floor must be rejected when entered.

### BR-09: Bidding Increment
The bidding increment is ₹5 per quintal for commodities priced below ₹3,000 per quintal and ₹10 per quintal for commodities priced above ₹3,000 per quintal.

The exact increment at ₹3,000 must be clarified with the organizers.

### BR-10: Bid Increment Validation
A bid must satisfy the required increment relative to the current highest valid bid. Invalid bids must be rejected.

### BR-11: Auction Duration
An auction's initial bidding window is ten minutes.

### BR-12: Auction Extension
A bid arriving during the final 60 seconds triggers a two-minute extension, provided fewer than three extensions have already been used.

### BR-13: Maximum Extensions
An auction may have at most three extensions. No additional extension is permitted after the third extension expires.

### BR-14: Winner Selection
The highest valid bid wins, provided the trader is eligible and all award conditions are satisfied.

### BR-15: Tie-Breaking
If the highest bids are identical, the earliest timestamp wins. If timestamps are identical to the millisecond, the lower trader ID wins.

### BR-16: Unsold Lots
If no valid bid is available when an auction closes, the lot is marked UNSOLD and scheduled for re-auction the following day.

### BR-17: Re-Auction Floor
The re-auction floor is reduced by 2% at each re-auction, but must never fall below 80% of reference price.

The organizers should clarify whether this means a two-percentage-point reduction from reference price or a 2% reduction from the previous floor.

### BR-18: Withdrawal Before Auction
A farmer may withdraw a lot before its auction window opens without a withdrawal penalty.

### BR-19: Withdrawal After Bidding Begins
If bids already exist, a permitted farmer withdrawal incurs a penalty equal to 2% of the lot's reference value.

### BR-20: Withdrawal After Award
A farmer cannot withdraw a lot after it has been awarded to a trader.

## C. Trader Eligibility and Credit Exposure

### BR-21: Credit Limit Enforcement
A trader must not exceed the applicable credit limit when placing a bid.

### BR-22: Exposure Calculation
The eligibility check must include outstanding dues, the value of lots the trader is currently winning, and the proposed bid value.

The precise accounting treatment of lots already included in exposure must be defined to avoid double counting.

### BR-23: Overdue Dues Restriction
A trader with dues more than seven days overdue is barred from bidding.

### BR-24: Trader Status and Ledger
The system must track trader dues, payments, security deposit, penalties, and suspension status.

## D. Weigh-In and Settlement

### BR-25: Settlement Weight
Auctions use declared weight. Financial settlement uses the actual weigh-bridge weight, subject to the permitted withdrawal rule.

### BR-26: Weight Variance Withdrawal
If actual weight differs from declared weight by more than 2%, the trader may withdraw within 30 minutes of weigh-in without penalty.

### BR-27: Exact 2% Variance
A weight difference of exactly 2% does not qualify for the withdrawal right specified in BR-26.

### BR-28: Moisture Deduction
Moisture above 12% causes a price deduction of 1% per percentage point above 12%, calculated pro rata.

### BR-29: Foreign-Matter Deduction
Foreign matter above 1% causes a weight deduction equal to the excess percentage.

### BR-30: Deduction Order
Quality deductions must be applied before calculating market fees and agent commission. The implementation must document how the moisture price deduction and foreign-matter weight deduction combine to determine settlement quantity and value.

### BR-31: Market Fee
The market fee is 1% of gross settlement value. The trader pays this fee on top of the bid value.

### BR-32: Agent Commission
Commission is 2% of gross settlement value, capped at ₹2,000 per lot, and deducted from the farmer's proceeds.

### BR-33: Direct Farmer Sale
If a lot is sold directly by the farmer without an agent, commission is zero.

### BR-34: Farmer Net Payable
Farmer net payable is calculated after the applicable quality deductions and agent commission. The final formula must reflect the agreed definition of gross settlement value.

### BR-35: Payment Deadline
The trader must pay the farmer's net payable into the market account within 24 hours of weigh-in.

### BR-36: Delayed Payment Penalty
Payment delay beyond 24 hours attracts a penalty of 1% of lot value per day for up to seven days.

The organizers should clarify how partial days are counted and which value defines lot value for the penalty.

### BR-37: Deposit Encashment and Suspension
After seven days of payment delay, the trader's security deposit is encashed and the trader is suspended.

### BR-38: Settlement Record
Each settlement must retain actual weight, deductions, gross value, fees, commission, net payable, weigh-in timestamp, payment deadline, payment history, penalties, and status.

## E. Bundling, Reporting, and Validation

### BR-39: Minimum Lot Size
Lots below five quintals must be bundled with other eligible lots belonging to the same farmer and grade.

### BR-40: Individual Bundle Settlement
Each component of a bundled lot must be settled individually by weight proportion.

The allocation method for indivisible currency fractions must be documented.

### BR-41: Component Withdrawal
If one component of a bundle is withdrawn after bidding begins, the system must handle the withdrawal penalty and the remaining components consistently.

The organizers should clarify whether the remaining components continue under the existing auction or require a revised auction.

### BR-42: Daily Auction Register
The system must print the daily auction register, including auction results, winning trader, price, and number of extensions.

### BR-43: Farmer Payment Advice
The system must generate a settlement advice for each farmer lot showing gross value, deductions, commission, net payable, and payment deadline.

### BR-44: Trader Ledger
The system must print trader transactions, purchases, fees, payments, dues, penalties, and applicable suspension or deposit-encashment entries.

### BR-45: Input Validation
Malformed, incomplete, duplicate, or inconsistent input records must be rejected or reported with clear validation errors.

### BR-46: Deterministic Results
Identical valid inputs must produce consistent auction decisions, monetary calculations, and report contents.

## F. Required Edge-Case Tests

The following cases must have explicit automated tests:

1. Moisture exactly 12.00% versus 12.01%.
2. A bid arriving exactly 60 seconds before the auction closes.
3. A bid arriving in the final second of the third extension.
4. Actual weight differing from declared weight by exactly 2.00%.
5. Commission reaching the ₹2,000 per-lot cap.
6. Payment made exactly 24 hours after weigh-in.
7. An unsold lot re-auctioned on day five when the floor reaches 80% of reference price.
8. One component of a bundled lot being withdrawn after bidding begins.
9. Equal highest bids with different timestamps.
10. Equal highest bids with identical timestamps to the millisecond.
11. A trader reaching the credit limit exactly.
12. A trader whose dues are more than seven days overdue.
13. Moisture, foreign matter, or damage exactly at grade boundaries.
14. A direct farmer sale without an agent.
15. Multiple quality deductions applied before fees and commission.

## G. Rule Governance

Each business rule must be connected to:
- Its implementation location.
- At least one automated test.
- Its expected result.
- Any relevant design decision.

Ambiguous requirements must be recorded in the decision log and clarified with the organizers wherever possible.

No implementation should silently substitute an assumption for an unresolved rule that can affect evaluation results.
